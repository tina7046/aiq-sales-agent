{# Cadence + email preview LAB ===================================================================== Standalone sandbox for redesigning the cadence card — the drafted sequence of touches, and the email preview inside each one — that the conversation feed renders in an agent response. Reachable at /cadence_lab.html. Nothing links to it and nothing in the app imports from it, so anything tried here is invisible to the demo until it's deliberately ported. Isolation is the point, and it's the same arrangement the prospect table lab uses. Every file here is a COPY of its production counterpart carrying the same class names, hooks and macro names: src/views/_cadence_lab_card.html ← _home_conversation_cadence.html src/styles/cadence_lab.scss ← home_conversation.scss, the live cadence / step / citation regions src/scripts/cadence_lab.js ← home_conversation_cadence.js src/views/data/cadence_lab.json ← index.json, conv-001's cadence object + prospect roster Two files are copies of something else — the Prospect and Cadence lookups in the card header, taken from hunter's Write Messaging step in protos/BB - AOP rather than from this app, which has no such control: src/views/_cadence_lab_lookups.html ← BB - AOP step_04_write_messaging.html src/scripts/cadence_lab_lookups.js ← BB - AOP app.js + cadence-popover.js Their popover styles are appended to the bottom of cadence_lab.scss from BB - AOP's step_04.scss, and the cadence list they offer is the `cadences` key in cadence_lab.json. The page deliberately does NOT load home_conversation.css: the copies use production class names, so loading both would make which rule wins depend on link order. What the page gives you that the thread doesn't ----------------------------------------------- A width stage. The card is a 7/5 two-column split inside the thread's 800px column, which leaves ~421px for the draft and ~301px for the Details panel — tight enough that width is the first thing any redesign has to be judged at. The toolbar sets one custom property on the stage (`--cl-stage-width`) and reports what the card actually measured, so "does this survive at 800?" is a click rather than a window resize. What it doesn't have: the prospect table. In the app a row click selects a prospect and the card follows, over `sh:conv-row-select`. Here the Prospect lookup in the header does that instead — it re-announces a pick as the same event, so the card can't tell the difference. The Cadence lookup only sets its own chip. The lab holds one cadence's worth of drafted touches (37 sequences under "High Touch Prospects"), so choosing "Enterprise Outbound" has nothing to swap the steps for. The three popover modes and the Generate prompt all work as interactions and record what was chosen on the wrapper (`data-lookup-action-variant`, `data-lookup-generate-prompt`); what they don't do is change the sequence below. Adding a variant ---------------- There's one card here, the current design. To try a second shape beside it, copy _cadence_lab_card.html to _cadence_lab_card_b.html, import it under a second name, and render it in its own `.cl-stage`. Two things to know before you do: the card mounts by `[data-sh-cadence-root]` and wires whatever it finds, so a second card on the page works without touching the script — but the approve-all dialog is found by searching the shared parent, so give each variant its own wrapper element or they'll fight over one dialog. To port a change back into the app, diff the lab copy against its source.
