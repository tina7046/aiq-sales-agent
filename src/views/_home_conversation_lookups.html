{# Prospect + Cadence lookups — ported from BB - AOP ===================================================================== The pair of fields at the top of hunter's Write Messaging step (protos/BB - AOP → src/views/step_04_write_messaging.html, lines 29–347). They were the one part of that step the conversation feed left behind — the thread had the prospect table and the cadence card's pager instead. Tried again in the cadence lab, they beat the pager, so they replaced it; this is that port. Imported by _home_conversation_cadence.html, which renders both fields in the card header. Two fields, same shell, different dropdowns: Prospect a plain SLDS lookup. Search input, a list of entity rows (icon, name, "Prospect • VP, Sales • Rippling"), and a chip with a clear X once you pick. Cadence the same field, but its dropdown is a popover rather than a list: a three-way mode picker across the top, and under it either a searchable cadence list (Use as-is, Optimize content) or a prompt textarea (Generate cadence). What's markup here and what isn't --------------------------------- Only the Cadence popover's contents are authored. Both fields' OPTION LISTS are rendered by home_conversation_lookups.js at init from `window.SH_PROSPECT_DATA` / `SH_CADENCE_DATA`, which is why the `
    ` in the Prospect field ships empty. Everything the script needs is on the wrapper as `data-lookup-*`; that contract is documented at the top of the script. Hunter hand-wrote the popover's four cadence rows twice, once per panel. Here they come off `cadence.cadences` in index.json and loop, so a fifth cadence is a line of data rather than sixteen lines of markup in two places. Ids: the mode radios are `cadence-mode-*`, and the panel-switching rule in home_conversation.scss matches them by id (`:has(#… :checked)`). That means ONE popover per page.
